Contact Us

Thank you for visiting UnblockedGames.free.

We're always working to improve our platform and provide a better browser gaming experience. Whether you have a question, want to report a technical issue, suggest a new game, or contact us about copyright matters, we'd be happy to hear from you.

To help us review your request more efficiently, please choose the category that best matches your inquiry.

Technical Support & Game Issues

If you're experiencing a problem while playing a game, please let us know.

Examples include:

  • A game won't load
  • Black or blank screen
  • Controls aren't working correctly
  • Performance or compatibility issues
  • Missing or incorrect game information
  • Broken links

Helpful Information to Include

To help us understand the issue, please include:

  • Game title
  • Device (Windows PC, Chromebook, Mac, Android, iPhone, etc.)
  • Browser name and version
  • A brief description of the issue
  • Screenshot (optional)

Support Email: support@unblockedgames.free

Game Suggestions

Know a great browser game that should be part of our collection?

We welcome suggestions from our community. If there's a browser game you'd like us to review, send us:

  • Game name
  • Official website or publisher (if available)
  • A short description of the game
  • Any additional information you'd like to share

Community suggestions help us continue expanding our game library.

Game Developers & Publishing Inquiries

If you're a game developer or publisher and would like us to review your browser game for possible inclusion on UnblockedGames.free, we'd love to hear from you.

When contacting us, please include:

  • Game title
  • Official game website
  • Supported platforms
  • Short description
  • Confirmation that you have the appropriate rights to distribute or publish the game

Developer Email: developers@unblockedgames.free

Submitting a game does not guarantee publication. Every submission is reviewed according to our editorial standards and platform requirements.

Copyright & DMCA Requests

We respect intellectual property rights and take copyright-related concerns seriously.

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that content on our website requires review, please provide:

  • Your name and contact information
  • The URL(s) involved
  • A description of the concern
  • Supporting documentation where applicable

Please also review our DMCA Policy before submitting a copyright request.

Legal Email: legal@unblockedgames.free

Business & Partnership Inquiries

For business-related questions, partnerships, advertising opportunities, or other professional inquiries, please contact us with details about your proposal.

Providing clear information helps us better understand your request.
