What Are Idle Games?

Idle games, also known as incremental games, are games where progress is made by collecting resources, earning currency, unlocking upgrades, and gradually expanding your progress. Many idle games continue generating rewards automatically, allowing players to return later and continue from where they left off.

Some idle games require frequent interaction, while others automate many tasks as new upgrades become available. This combination of planning and gradual progression makes the genre enjoyable for both casual and dedicated players.

Modern HTML5 idle games can usually be played instantly through supported browsers without additional software.
