What Are Multiplayer Games?

Multiplayer games are games that allow two or more players to participate in the same game session. Depending on the title, players may compete against each other, cooperate as teammates, or complete shared objectives together.

Some multiplayer games support local two-player gameplay on the same device, while others connect players through online servers for real-time competition. Many modern browser games combine multiplayer features with genres such as action, sports, racing, shooting, adventure, and strategy.

Thanks to HTML5 and other modern web technologies, many multiplayer games can be played directly in supported browsers without downloading additional software.

Multiplayer games include several different styles of gameplay.

Work together with teammates to solve puzzles, complete objectives, or survive difficult challenges.

  • Local Two-Player Games

Some games allow two players to share the same keyboard or device, making them ideal for playing with friends or family.

  • Online Multiplayer Games

Join players from different locations in real-time matches using supported online servers.
