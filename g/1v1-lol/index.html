1V1 Lol

Developer: JustPlay.LOL
Rating: 9.1/10
Released: December 2019
Game Engine: Unity WebGL
Platforms: Browser (desktop-only)
Orientation: Landscape
Action » Shooting » Multiplayer » IO »

1v1.LOL is an online third-person shooter where building plays a major role in every fight. Create walls, ramps, and platforms to defend yourself, reach better positions, and outplay opponents during matches.

The main mode focuses on battle royale-style combat where the last player alive wins the match. Fast building and accurate shooting are both important if you want to survive against skilled players.

Game Modes

There are several game modes available in 1v1.LOL:

  • BR Duos (2v2 Battle Royale) – Team up with another player and fight against other squads.
  • 1v1 Mode – Face a single opponent in a direct build-and-shoot battle.
  • Box 2v2 – Play fast close-range matches with boxed-in combat arenas.
  • You can also try additional modes such as:
  • JustBuild – Practice building techniques without combat.
  • Aim Trainer – Improve your aiming and weapon accuracy.
  • Party Mode – Create private matches or join games with friends.

How to Play

The way you play depends on the selected mode, but most matches focus on survival combat. Build structures quickly for protection and movement while attacking opponents with your weapons. Stay alert, position yourself carefully, and eliminate other players to become the final survivor.

Game Features

  • Build walls, ramps, and platforms during battles
  • Practice aiming and construction separately
  • Play private matches with friends
  • Customize controls to match your playstyle
  • Unlock and purchase cosmetic items

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • W / A / S / D / Arrow Keys – Move
  • Space – Jump
  • C – Crouch
  • Z / X / C / V / Y – Switch building platforms
  • F / 1 / 2 – Switch weapons
  • R – Reload weapon / Rotate structure
  • G – Edit structure
  • E – Interact / Use item
  • Q – Switch weapon / Pickaxe

Mouse Controls

  • Left Mouse Button – Shoot / Place structure
  • Right Mouse Button – Aim down sights / Zoom

FAQ

Is JustBuild.lol the same as 1v1.lol?

JustBuild.lol is the building-only version of the game and does not include combat.

Is 1v1.lol similar to Fortnite?

The game shares similar building and shooting mechanics, but it is designed to run directly in a web browser with a simpler style.

Can I play 1v1.lol on mobile?

Yes, the game is available on both Android and iOS devices in addition to browser play.

Does 1v1.lol have a Battle Royale mode?

Yes, there is a Battle Royale mode that supports up to 10 players, along with other multiplayer modes featuring team-based matches.
