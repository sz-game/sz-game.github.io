3D Bowling

Developer: Famobi
Rating: 8.1/10
Released: January 2017
Game Engine: HTML5
Platforms: Browser (desktop, mobile, tablet)
Orientation: Landscape
Sports »

3D Bowling is a realistic bowling sports game where you can practice your skills solo or compete against another player. The detailed bowling alley visuals create the feeling of being at a real bowling center, from the polished lanes to the familiar atmosphere around you.

Gameplay

Before starting a match, choose whether you want to play 5 or 10 rounds. After that, select your opponent’s difficulty level. Higher difficulties make the computer player more accurate and harder to beat.

During your turn, move the bowling ball left or right to line up your shot. Pull your arm back and release the ball toward the pins. If you do not score a strike on your first throw, you get a second attempt to knock down the remaining pins.

Scoring depends on how many pins you clear each round. Strikes give the highest score, while clearing all remaining pins on the second throw also rewards bonus points. The player with the highest total score at the end of the match wins the game.

You can play against the computer or challenge another player to see who is the better bowler.

How to Play

Choose your preferred game mode before starting the match. Use your mouse to aim the bowling ball, adjust its spin, and control the shot power. Release the ball at the right moment to send it down the lane and knock over the pins. Try to score strikes by clearing all ten pins in a single throw across 10 frames. Accurate timing and proper ball control will help you achieve higher scores. For better shots, release the ball when the power meter is near its highest point.

Controls

  • Mouse – Drag the left mouse button to aim, swing, and release the ball to throw it.

Features

  • 3D Bowling Graphics – Play on detailed bowling lanes with realistic visuals that recreate the feel of a real bowling alley.
  • Multiple Game Modes – Choose between solo play, competing against the computer, or challenging a friend in local multiplayer matches.
  • Smooth Ball Physics – Enjoy natural bowling mechanics that make every throw feel responsive and accurate.
  • Mid-Shot Ball Control – Slightly adjust the ball’s direction after releasing it to improve your chances of scoring strikes.

Developer

  • 3D Bowling was developed by Famobi.
