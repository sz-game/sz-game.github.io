Temple of Boom

Temple of Boom is an action-packed platform shooter where you battle waves of enemies inside dangerous temple arenas. Armed with powerful weapons such as shotguns, machine guns, and rocket launchers, you must jump, dodge, and fight to survive. Play alone or team up with a friend in local multiplayer for even more chaotic action.

The game combines fast shooting mechanics with platform-based movement, creating intense battles where quick reflexes and smart positioning are essential.

Gameplay

Fight your way through enemy-filled temples while collecting stronger weapons and avoiding incoming attacks. Weapons appear throughout the arena, allowing you to upgrade your firepower and adapt to different combat situations.

Every battle requires careful movement, accurate shooting, and good timing to stay alive.

Game Modes

  • Campaign Mode

Battle through three ancient temples filled with monsters and dangerous creatures. Complete levels and survive increasingly difficult encounters.

  • Endless Mode

Fight endless waves of enemies and see how long you can survive. The challenge grows tougher as more enemies appear.

  • 1v1 Battle

Compete against a friend in local multiplayer matches where only one player can win.

  • Co-op Mode

Team up with a friend and work together to defeat enemies and survive longer.

How to Play

Move around the arena, collect weapons, and eliminate enemies while avoiding incoming attacks. Weapons appear throughout each level, giving you access to shotguns, machine guns, rocket launchers, and more.

Use double jumps to reach higher platforms and dodge enemy fire. Survival depends on quick reactions, smart positioning, and making the best use of the weapons you find.

Controls

Single Player

  • Up Arrow — Jump / Double Jump
  • Left / Right Arrow — Move
  • Down Arrow — Pick Up Weapon
  • Z — Shoot
  • X — Switch Weapon

Player 1

  • W — Jump / Double Jump
  • A / D — Move
  • S — Pick Up Weapon
  • C — Shoot
  • V — Switch Weapon

Player 2

  • Up Arrow — Jump / Double Jump
  • Left / Right Arrow — Move
  • Down Arrow — Pick Up Weapon
  • K — Shoot
  • L — Switch Weapon

Weapons

Collect powerful weapons throughout the game, including:

  • Pistols
  • Shotguns
  • Machine Guns
  • Rocket Launchers
  • Special Weapon Pickups

Characters

  • Player 1

A temple explorer equipped to battle monsters and survive dangerous arenas.

  • Player 2

A second adventurer who can join in co-op or competitive matches.

Features

  • Fast-paced platform shooter gameplay
  • Multiple weapon types to collect
  • Campaign and Endless modes
  • Local multiplayer support
  • Co-op and competitive gameplay
  • Double-jump movement mechanics
  • Challenging enemy waves
  • Retro-inspired pixel graphics

Tips

  • Keep moving to avoid enemy attacks.
  • Grab stronger weapons whenever they appear.
  • Use double jumps to reach safer positions.
  • Save powerful weapons for larger enemy groups.
  • In co-op mode, work together to control different areas of the arena.
  • Learn enemy movement patterns to survive longer.

FAQ

What is Temple of Boom?

Temple of Boom is a platform shooting game where players fight enemies inside temple arenas using a variety of weapons.

Can I play Temple of Boom with a friend?

Yes. The game supports local multiplayer, allowing two players to play together on the same device.

What game modes are available?

The game features Campaign Mode, Endless Mode, and multiplayer battles.

What weapons can I use?

You can collect and use weapons such as shotguns, machine guns, rocket launchers, and other powerful firearms.

Is Temple of Boom a single-player game?

No. While it can be played solo, it also includes multiplayer and co-op gameplay options.

How do I switch weapons?

Press X in single-player mode, V for Player 1, or L for Player 2.

What is Endless Mode?

Endless Mode challenges players to survive against continuous waves of enemies for as long as possible.

Is Temple of Boom free to play?

Yes. You can play Temple of Boom directly in your browser without downloading or installing anything.
