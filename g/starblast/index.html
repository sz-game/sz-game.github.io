Star Blast

StarBlast is a multiplayer space shooter where you control a customizable spaceship, mine asteroids for resources, and battle other players across the galaxy. As you collect crystals, you can upgrade your ship with stronger weapons, better defenses, and improved performance. Teamwork plays a key role, allowing players to join forces and fight for dominance in large-scale space battles.

Game Modes

  • Team Mode – Join one of three teams, mine crystals, upgrade your ship, and work together to destroy enemy bases.
  • Survival Mode – Fight every player for yourself, collect crystals, upgrade your ship, and be the last pilot standing.
  • Invasion – Cooperate with other players to defend your base against waves of AI alien ships.
  • Pro Deathmatch – A fast-paced free-for-all mode with instant access to advanced ships and intense PvP combat.
  • Custom Games (Modding Space) – Create or join community-made games with custom rules, maps, and gameplay modifications.

How to Play

In StarBlast, your objective is to collect crystals by destroying asteroids and enemy ships. These crystals can be used to upgrade your spacecraft, unlock new ships, and purchase extra lives.

Training

Before entering online battles, complete the training mode to learn the basics. The tutorial teaches you how to:

  • Mine asteroids
  • Fight enemy ships
  • Collect crystals
  • Upgrade your spacecraft

Choose a Game Mode

StarBlast offers four game modes:

  • Team Mode
  • Survival Mode
  • Invasion
  • Pro Deathmatch

For new players, Team Mode is a great starting point because teammates can help you during battles.

Factions

Choose one of three factions:

  • Federation
  • Sovereign Trappist Colonies
  • Orion Arm Guild

Mine Crystals

Crystals are the primary resource in the game. You can earn them by:

  • Mining asteroids
  • Destroying enemy ships

Target asteroids that match your ship's size and firepower to collect crystals more efficiently.

Upgrade Your Ship

Spend crystals to improve your ship's abilities. Crystals can also be converted into credits, which can be used to purchase weapons and equipment at your team's base.

Available Upgrades

  • Shield Capacity
  • Shield Regeneration
  • Energy Capacity
  • Energy Regeneration
  • Laser Damage
  • Weapon Speed
  • Ship Speed
  • Ship Agility

Ship Progression

  • Unlock up to 7 ship tiers
  • Increase cargo capacity
  • Access 31 unique ships with different strengths and playstyles

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • Arrow Keys / WASD – Move and steer your ship
  • Space Bar – Fire weapons
  • Alt – Fire secondary weapon
  • W – Open secondary weapon shop (Survival mode)
  • U – Open/close upgrade bar
  • R – Toggle radar
  • C – Toggle chat commands
  • Tab – Toggle leaderboard
  • Z – Take a screenshot
  • V – Give gems to teammates (Team Mode)
  • A / D – Strafe left / right (when enabled in custom games)

Mouse Controls

  • Mouse Movement – Aim and steer your ship
  • Left Mouse Button – Fire weapons
  • Right Mouse Button – Move/steer (hold and drag)
  • Scroll Wheel – Select secondary weapons

Developer

FAQ

Is StarBlast Free to Play?

Yes, StarBlast is free to play. Optional premium features are available through the Elite Commander Pass (ECP).

Can I Play With Friends?

Yes. Share a room link with your friends to join the same server and play together.

How Do I Get More Weapons and Ammo?

Use credits to buy additional weapons from the weapon store. In Survival Mode, press W to open it.

How Do I Upgrade My Ship?

Collect enough crystals to unlock upgrades. Fill your crystal cargo and return to your base to access higher-tier ships.
