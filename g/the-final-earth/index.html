The Final Earth

Developer: Florian van Strien

Released: June 2019
Game Engine: HTML5
Platforms: Browser (desktop, mobile, tablet)

The Final Earth

The Final Earth is a city-building and colony management game set on a new planet where humanity must start over. Your goal is to gather resources, construct buildings, and develop a thriving settlement capable of supporting a growing population.

You begin with basic resource collection, including wood, stone, and food, which are essential for expanding your colony. As your city grows, you unlock new structures that help improve efficiency, increase population, and maintain the well-being of your settlers. Careful planning and resource management are required to ensure your colony remains stable and continues to expand.

Game Modes

  • Scenarios (Story Mode) – Complete predefined challenges with unique objectives, limited resources, and specific victory conditions.
  • Free Play – Build and expand your space colony without fixed story objectives, managing resources and population at your own pace.
  • Sandbox Mode – Create a colony with customizable settings, unlimited resources, and no restrictions for experimentation and creative building.

Instructions

Your main objective is to build and manage a successful colony on a new planet. Start by collecting essential resources such as food, wood, and stone, which are needed for construction and expansion. Resource levels can be monitored in the bottom-right corner of the screen.

Begin by constructing a stone mine and a woodcutting center using the build menu located in the bottom-left corner. Place these buildings on suitable open ground connected to your starting area.

After establishing basic production, expand your colony by building houses to increase population and farms to secure a stable food supply. As your population grows, more advanced buildings become available.

Carefully balance resource production, housing, and food supply to keep your colony stable and progressing efficiently.

How to Play

  • Start by gathering basic resources like wood, stone, and food to support your colony.
  • Build essential production buildings such as a woodcutting center and a stone mine to collect materials automatically.
  • Expand your colony by constructing houses to increase population and farms to produce food.
  • Keep track of your resources to make sure your colony stays stable and well-fed.
  • Continue expanding with new buildings as your population grows and more options become available.
  • Balance production and consumption carefully to avoid shortages and keep your citizens happy.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • Ctrl + Click – Follow an entity
  • Esc / Right Click – Deselect buildings or exit menus

Mouse Controls

  • Left Mouse Button – Select, place, or interact with buildings and UI
  • Right Mouse Button – Deselect current tool or building
  • Scroll Wheel – Zoom in and out
  • Drag – Navigate the map or use special build modes like "Multi Replace"

FAQs

What is The Final Earth?

The Final Earth is a colony-building game where you manage resources, construct buildings, and develop a new civilization on a different planet after Earth’s destruction.

What is the main goal of the game?

Your main goal is to build a stable and growing colony by gathering resources, constructing essential buildings, and keeping your population fed and satisfied.

How do I get resources in The Final Earth?

You can gather resources by building structures like a woodcutting center for wood and a stone mine for stone. These buildings produce resources automatically over time.

How do I increase my population?

You can increase your population by building houses, which allow more citizens to join your colony and help expand production.

How do I keep my colony stable?

You need to balance resource production and consumption. Make sure you have enough food, wood, and stone to support your population and continue expanding.

What happens if I run out of food?

If food runs out, your colony may become unstable, so it’s important to build farms early and maintain a steady food supply.

Is The Final Earth easy to play?

Yes, the controls are simple and mostly mouse-based, but managing resources and planning expansion requires strategy and careful decision-making.
