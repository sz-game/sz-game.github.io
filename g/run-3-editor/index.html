Run 3 Editor

Run 3 Editor is a creative browser-based game that combines fast-paced running with level design tools. Players can build their own custom stages and test them within the familiar space-themed runner environment. It is a great option for players who enjoy creating challenges, experimenting with different layouts, and improving their platforming skills through user-made levels.

The original game that inspired this editor was created by a solo developer known as player_03 and was first released around 2014. This editor version is primarily designed for web browsers, allowing players to create and play custom levels without downloading or installing anything. Its browser-based accessibility makes it easy to jump in, start building, and share new ideas with others.

Gameplay

Run 3 Editor lets players create and test custom levels with ease. In Editor Mode, you can place tunnel pieces, gravity tiles, gaps, and obstacles to design your own course. Once finished, switch to Play Mode and test your creation instantly.

Players control an alien runner using simple controls, jumping and changing gravity to avoid hazards and stay on the path. A level is completed by reaching the end successfully, while falling into space or missing a jump results in a restart.

The difficulty depends entirely on your design choices. You can build simple beginner-friendly stages or challenging courses filled with tricky jumps and gravity shifts. The main reward comes from creating, improving, and completing your own unique levels, giving the game plenty of replay value.

How to Play

  • Open Editor Mode – Select the Editor option to start creating your own level.
  • Build the Course – Drag and place tunnel sections, platforms, gaps, and gravity tiles to design a custom path.
  • Add Obstacles – Include jumps, broken sections, and gravity changes to make the level more challenging.
  • Test Your Level – Switch to Play Mode to see how your creation works.
  • Reach the Finish – Control your character, avoid hazards, and complete the course without falling.
  • Edit and Improve – Return to the editor anytime to fix issues, adjust difficulty, or add new ideas.
  • Create More Levels – Experiment with different layouts and challenges to build unique courses and improve your designs.

Controls

PC / Laptop

  • A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys – Move left or right
  • Spacebar or Up Arrow – Jump or switch gravity
  • Mouse – Place, move, and edit level pieces in Editor Mode
  • P / Esc – Pause the game
  • R – Restart the current level

Mobile / Tablet

  • Tap Left / Right Side – Move left or right
  • Tap Screen – Jump or flip gravity
  • Drag and Tap – Build and edit levels in Editor Mode
  • Pause Button – Pause the game or open the menu

Tips and Tricks

  • Start with simple level designs before creating complex courses.
  • Test your levels often to find mistakes and improve the layout.
  • Look ahead to upcoming obstacles instead of focusing only on your character.
  • Use gravity changes to explore different paths and avoid hazards.
  • Practice difficult sections until you can complete them consistently.
  • Stay calm during fast-paced runs and focus on timing.
  • Learn the correct jump timing for different gaps and obstacles.
  • Keep your level challenging but fair for players to complete.
  • Experiment with different tunnel layouts and gravity mechanics.
  • Study successful level designs to get ideas for your own creations.
