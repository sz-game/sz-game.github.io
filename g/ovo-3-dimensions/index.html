OvO 3 : Dimension

OvO 3 : Dimension is a fast-paced arcade platformer created by Dedra Games. You control a stickman character using simple movement mechanics as you jump, wall jump, and slide through increasingly challenging levels.

Although the controls are easy to learn, mastering movement combinations is the key to progressing faster. Chaining actions together, such as jumping immediately after a slide or ground slam, can give you extra momentum and help you clear difficult obstacles more efficiently.With plenty of levels to complete, each stage introduces new platforming challenges that test your timing and movement. The question is—can you make it through every level?

How to Play

Start by learning the basic controls and movement mechanics shown inside the game. Move through each level, avoid hazards, and reach the finish as quickly as possible.

Combine different actions to maintain momentum. Jumping right after a slide or bounce can help you cover longer distances, while wall jumps are useful for climbing vertical areas and reaching hidden paths.

Collect optional coins along the way and replay levels to improve your speed and performance.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • Left / Right Arrow Keys – Move
  • Up Arrow Key – Jump
  • Down Arrow Key – Slide (while moving) / Ground Smash (while in the air)
  • Up Arrow Key (while touching a wall) – Wall Jump
