Rabbit Samurai 2

Rabbit Samurai 2 is a delightful physics-based platformer where momentum plays a key role in every move. Launch your rabbit using a grappling hook, attach to honey wheels, swing past obstacles, collect carrots, and reach the exit portal. The levels are short, easy to replay, and ideal for both casual play and speedrunning. With smooth controls, responsive swinging mechanics, and plenty of collectibles to chase, the game offers a rewarding experience directly in your browser.

Gameplay

Each level is built around a compact obstacle course filled with rotating honey wheels and anchor points. Your rabbit fires a rope toward nearby anchors, allowing you to swing, gain speed, and navigate the stage. The objective is to maintain momentum, avoid hazards, gather carrots, and reach the portal as efficiently as possible.

As you progress, you'll learn to master:

  • Precise aiming to connect with the right anchor quickly
  • Swing timing to gain height and travel farther
  • Momentum management to maintain speed between swings
  • High-risk routes that reward skilled play with better scores

The game is easy to pick up but offers plenty of depth for players aiming for perfect runs and faster completion times.

Game Modes

  • Classic Levels

Collect carrots and reach the portal. Earn up to three stars by completing levels efficiently.

  • Speedrun Challenges

Replay stages to improve your times and discover faster routes.

  • Optional Challenge Routes

Take more dangerous paths featuring tighter anchor placements and additional hazards for greater rewards.

How to Play

  1. Start the game in your browser and enter the first level.
  2. Aim your grappling hook toward a honey wheel or anchor point.
  3. Swing to build momentum and release at the right moment.
  4. Collect every carrot you can before reaching the portal.
  5. Restart instantly whenever you want to improve your route or score.
  6. Continue through new worlds featuring increasingly complex obstacles.

Controls

Mouse or Touch

  • Click, tap, or hold to attach to anchors and swing
  • Release to launch your rabbit forward

Keyboard

  • Arrow Keys / WASD – Adjust aim
  • Spacebar – Attach grappling hook

Mobile

  • Tap reachable anchors
  • Swipe aiming may be available on supported devices

Other

  • R Key or Reset Button – Restart the level instantly

Tips & Tricks

Beginner Tips

  • Connect to nearby anchors first for better control.
  • Release near the top of a swing to maximize distance.
  • Plan your carrot collection route to avoid backtracking.
  • Use quick restarts to practice difficult sections efficiently.

Advanced Strategies

  • Chain multiple swings together to maintain momentum.
  • Use lower swing arcs to pass safely beneath hazards.
  • Aim for your next anchor before landing to save time.
  • Experiment with different routes to find the fastest path.
