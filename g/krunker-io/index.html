Krunker.io

Krunker.io is a multiplayer 3D FPS where players compete in fast-paced online battles to achieve the highest score before the match ends. Every game demands quick reactions, accurate aim, and constant movement as you face opponents from around the world.

The game features multiple character classes, each equipped with a unique weapon and playstyle. Whether you prefer the versatility of the Triggerman's assault rifle, the close-range firepower of the Run N Gun's SMG, or the precision of the Hunter's sniper rifle, there's a class suited to every type of player.

How to Play Krunker.io

Success in Krunker.io depends on staying alert and reacting quickly to enemy movements. Move carefully through the maps, listen for nearby gunfire, and learn the layout of each battlefield to gain a tactical advantage.

Understanding map routes, common combat zones, and strategic positions can help you avoid ambushes and improve your chances of securing more eliminations. Fast decision-making and accurate shooting are essential if you want to dominate matches and climb the leaderboard.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • W / A / S / D – Move Forward, Strafe Left, Move Backward, Strafe Right
  • Space – Jump
  • Shift – Crouch
  • R – Reload
  • E – Switch to Secondary Weapon
  • Q – Switch to Melee Weapon
  • 1, 2, 3 – Killstreaks

Mouse Controls

  • Mouse Movement – Look and Aim
  • Left Mouse Button – Primary Fire
  • Right Mouse Button – Aim Down Sights (ADS)

Character Classes

Krunker.io offers a wide selection of classes, each falling into one of three categories based on mobility, durability, and weapon type.

Light Classes

Light classes focus on speed and agility but have lower health.

  • Agent
  • Hunter
  • Run N Gun

Medium Classes

Medium classes provide a balanced combination of mobility, firepower, and survivability.

  • Bowman
  • Commando
  • Detective
  • Triggerman
  • Marksman
  • Vince

Heavy Classes

Heavy classes deal significant damage and can absorb more punishment but move more slowly.

  • Spray N Pray
  • Rocketeer

Each class offers a distinct combat experience, encouraging players to experiment and find the loadout that best matches their preferred strategy.

Game Modes

  • Free For All (FFA) – Every player fights independently, and the highest score wins.
  • Team Deathmatch (TDM) – Two teams compete to earn the most eliminations.
  • Capture the Flag (CTF) – Steal the enemy team's flag while defending your own.
  • Hardpoint (POINT) – Capture and hold the objective zone to score points for your team.
  • Infected – One player starts infected and must infect all remaining survivors.
  • Parkour – Complete movement-based obstacle courses instead of combat.
  • Ranked Mode – Competitive matchmaking with skill-based rankings and seasonal progression.

FAQ

How Can I Play Krunker.io for Free?

You can play Krunker.io for free directly in your web browser without downloading or installing anything.

Who Created Krunker.io?

Krunker.io was created by Sidney de Vries and developed by Yendis Entertainment.

Can I Play Krunker.io on Mobile and Desktop?

Krunker.io is primarily designed for desktop browsers. A mobile version has existed, but the best gameplay experience is on a computer with a keyboard and mouse.

Is Krunker.io a Multiplayer Game?

Yes. Krunker.io is an online multiplayer FPS where you compete against players from around the world in real time.

Does Krunker.io Have Different Game Modes?

Yes. It features multiple game modes, including Free for All, Team Deathmatch, Hardpoint, Capture the Flag, and several community-created custom modes.

Can I Create or Join Custom Games?

Yes. Krunker.io lets players create and join custom lobbies with unique maps, game settings, and community-made content.
