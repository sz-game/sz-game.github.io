Furious Racing 3D

Furious Racing 3D is a high-speed driving game that puts you behind the wheel of powerful supercars as you race through busy highways and scenic environments. Drive through the streets of Japan or the snowy mountain roads while weaving through traffic, earning rewards, and unlocking faster vehicles. With an immersive cockpit view, multiple race modes, and nitro boosts, every run tests your driving precision and reaction speed.

Traffic isn't the only challenge you'll face. Reckless driving can attract police attention, leading to intense chases where you'll need to use your speed and driving skills to escape. The faster you drive and the longer you survive, the greater your rewards and progression.

How to Play

  • Choose a track and game mode.
  • Accelerate through traffic while avoiding collisions.
  • Earn points and rewards by driving skillfully and covering long distances.
  • Use nitro boosts to reach higher speeds when needed.
  • Avoid police pursuit and maintain control of your vehicle.
  • Unlock new cars by collecting enough in-game currency.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • Up Arrow – Accelerate
  • Down Arrow – Brake
  • Left Arrow – Turn left
  • Right Arrow – Turn right
  • Space – Handbrake
  • X – Nitro boost
  • C – Switch camera view

Game Modes

  • One-Way

Drive through traffic moving in a single direction and cover as much distance as possible.

  • Two-Way

Navigate traffic coming from both directions, increasing the challenge and reward potential.

  • Time Attack

Race against the clock and reach checkpoints before time runs out.

  • Free Ride

Explore the track without time limits and enjoy a more relaxed driving experience.

Tracks

  • Japan

Race through busy city highways filled with traffic and sharp driving challenges.

  • Snow

Take on snowy roads and winter scenery while maintaining control at high speeds.

Features

  • Fast-paced highway racing gameplay
  • Multiple supercars to unlock
  • Nitro boost system for extra speed
  • First-person and alternative camera views
  • Dynamic traffic and police chases
  • Two unique environments to race in
  • Multiple game modes for different play styles

FAQ

How do I use nitro in Furious Racing 3D?

Press the X key to activate nitro. Watch the boost meter and use it strategically for maximum benefit.

What is the fastest car in the game?

One of the top-performing vehicles is inspired by the Bugatti Veyron. It can be unlocked by earning enough diamonds through gameplay.

Can I change my car's color?

Yes, you can customize your vehicle's color from the garage menu using the paint option.

What game modes are available?

The game features One-Way, Two-Way, Time Attack, and Free Ride modes.

Does Furious Racing 3D include police chases?

Yes, driving aggressively can attract police attention, leading to high-speed pursuits that add extra challenge to your races.
