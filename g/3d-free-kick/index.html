3D Free Kick

Developer: Famobi
Rating: 8.4/10
Released: June 2018
Game Engine: HTML5
Platforms: Browser (desktop, mobile, tablet)
Orientation: Landscape and Portrait
Skill » Sports »

3D Free Kick is a soccer free-kick game where accuracy and timing are the key to scoring goals. Take shots from different positions on the field while dealing with tougher obstacles as the game progresses. Each level becomes more challenging, starting with open shots before adding goalkeepers and defensive walls that make scoring more difficult.

The game features detailed 3D visuals and realistic ball movement that make every shot feel smooth and responsive. You only have three attempts to score, so every kick matters. As the pressure increases, you’ll need to carefully control your aim, power, and curve to keep your run going.

Gameplay

Your objective is to score as many free kicks as possible before missing three shots. The challenge gradually increases with moving goalkeepers, defensive walls, and tighter shooting angles that require better precision and control.

Each successful goal adds to your score and pushes you into harder situations where accuracy becomes even more important. Quick reactions and smart shot placement are the best ways to stay in the game longer.

How to Play

Click and drag the ball toward the goal to choose your shot direction and power. Release to kick the ball toward the net. You can also slightly curve or adjust the ball while it is moving through the air to avoid defenders and the goalkeeper.

The controls are easy to understand, but mastering curved shots and timing takes practice. Missing three goals ends the match, and your final score is recorded so you can try to beat it in the next round.

Control

Mouse Controls

  • Left Mouse Button (Click & Drag/Swipe) – Shoot the ball (controls shot speed, direction, and height)
  • Left Mouse Button (Swipe again mid-air) – Apply spin to curve the ball

Touch Controls

  • Swipe/Flick – Launch the ball towards the goal
  • Swipe again mid-air – Bend or curve the ball's trajectory

Game Features

  • Realistic 3D soccer graphics
  • Smooth ball physics and curved shot controls
  • Increasing difficulty with goalkeepers and defenders
  • Simple drag-and-shoot gameplay
  • High-score based progression system
  • Quick matches that are easy to replay
