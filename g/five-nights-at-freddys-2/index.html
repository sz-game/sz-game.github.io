Five Nights at Freddy’s 2

Five Nights at Freddy’s 2 brings back the horror with another night shift inside a creepy pizza restaurant. After the previous guard quit, you must survive five nights while watching security cameras and stopping animatronics from entering your office through the hallways and air vents.

The game adds new mechanics, updated characters, extra animations, and a flashlight to help you survive in the dark. Stay alert, manage your tools carefully, and make it through each terrifying night until morning. Scott Cawthon

Gameplay

In Five Nights at Freddy’s 2, you must survive five nights inside a haunted pizza restaurant while keeping animatronics away from your office. Use the security cameras to track their movement, watch the air vents on both sides, and manage your flashlight carefully. Quick reactions and constant attention are important because the animatronics can appear at any moment. Scott Cawthon

What Is FNAF 2?

FNAF 2, also known as Five Nights at Freddy’s 2, is a survival horror game created by Scott Cawthon. You play as a night security guard at Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza, where haunted animatronics roam the building after dark.

The game focuses on survival, quick reactions, and managing limited tools. Players must monitor security cameras, use the flashlight, wear the Freddy mask at the right time, and keep the Music Box running to stop animatronics from attacking. Its creepy atmosphere, sudden jump scares, and nonstop pressure made the game one of the most popular horror titles in the series.

How to Play FNAF 2

Your objective is to survive from midnight until 6 a.m. across five dangerous nights inside the restaurant.

To stay alive:

  • Watch the security cameras often
  • Keep the Music Box wound up
  • Use the flashlight to stop Foxy in the hallway
  • Wear the Freddy mask when animatronics enter the office
  • Listen carefully for audio clues and movement

Each night becomes harder as more animatronics appear and move faster, forcing you to react quickly and manage your tools carefully.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • Ctrl – Flash the hallway flashlight
  • Space – Open or close the camera monitor
  • Ctrl + Left Click (on screen buttons) – Interact with mask and cameras

Mouse Controls

  • Mouse Movement – Look around the office
  • Left Mouse Button – Interact with cameras, mask, vent lights, and flashlight buttons

Tips for Surviving Longer

  1. Always keep the Music Box active to prevent dangerous attacks
  2. Use the flashlight carefully instead of wasting it constantly
  3. Put on the Freddy mask immediately when animatronics appear
  4. Learn the different sound cues to react faster
  5. Stay calm during jump scares and avoid panicking

Horror Game Features of FNAF 2

New Animatronics Added

FNAF 2 introduces several new animatronics, each with unique attack patterns and behaviors that players must learn to survive.

  1. Toy Freddy
  2. Toy Bonnie
  3. Toy Chica
  4. Mangle
  5. Balloon Boy
  6. The Puppet
  7. Withered Foxy

Every character reacts differently, making each night more unpredictable and difficult.

Freddy Mask Mechanic

Instead of using security doors like the first game, players must wear the Freddy Fazbear mask to fool certain animatronics and force them to leave the office.

Music Box System

The Puppet can only be stopped by regularly winding the Music Box through Cam 11. Forgetting to maintain it for too long usually leads to an instant attack.

Flashlight Survival Gameplay

The flashlight is essential for checking dark hallways and defending against enemies like Foxy. However, Balloon Boy can disable the flashlight completely, making later nights even more stressful.

FAQ

Where can I play Five Nights at Freddy's 2 online?

You can play Five Nights at Freddy’s 2 for free directly in your desktop browser without downloading or installing anything.

How long is one night in Five Nights at Freddy's 2?

Each night lasts about six minutes in real time, representing the hours from 12 AM to 6 AM in the game.

When should I use Freddy’s mask?

Use the Freddy mask when animatronics enter the vents or appear inside the office. Put it on quickly and wait until they leave before removing it.

What happens if the battery dies?

If your power runs out, the office goes dark and you lose access to cameras and defenses, leaving you vulnerable to attacks.

How many animatronics are in Five Nights at Freddy's 2?

The game features multiple animatronics spread across different camera areas, each with unique movement patterns and attack behaviors players must learn to survive.
