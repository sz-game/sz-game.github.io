3 Pandas In Japan

Developer: flashteam games
Rating: N/A
Released: December 30, 2014
Game Engine: HTML5
Platforms: Browser (desktop, mobile, tablet)
Orientation: Landscape
Adventure » Puzzle »

Play 3 Pandas in Japan online and help three funny bears escape guards, ninjas, and tricky machines in a clever point-and-click puzzle adventure. This free browser game takes you through busy city streets and peaceful gardens filled with small puzzles that rely on teamwork and timing. If you want an unblocked puzzle game that works directly in your browser with no download required, this adventure offers quick challenges, fun animations, and easy-to-learn controls. Click or tap to move the pandas, activate devices, and stack them together to reach hidden switches and new paths. Guide the trio through Japan and solve each scene one puzzle at a time.

Gameplay

The gameplay focuses on observation, timing, and using each panda’s special ability to clear obstacles. The small panda can squeeze through narrow spaces, the middle panda helps with throwing and boosting actions, while the large panda uses strength to move heavy objects and support the team. You will activate platforms, avoid guards, climb to higher areas, and solve sequence-based puzzles by choosing the right panda for each task.

Game Modes

  • Story Adventure – Guide the three pandas through a series of connected puzzle levels as they escape danger and solve puzzles across Japan.

How to Play

  • Click or tap a panda to select it, then choose where it should move
  • Interact with switches, ropes, levers, and other objects to solve puzzles
  • Use each panda’s unique skill to overcome different obstacles
  • Avoid guards and hazards while guiding all three pandas safely to the exit
  • Try different movement orders or restart the scene if you get stuck

Controls

  • Mouse Controls

Left Mouse Button (Click) – Select a panda, direct them to walk or jump to a specific spot, trigger unique individual character skills, and interact with levers, buttons, or environmental items to solve puzzles.

  • Touch Controls

Tap – Touch a specific panda to select them, tap anywhere on the screen to guide their movement, activate special skills, or interact with mechanical switches and puzzle items.

Tips & Tricks

Beginner Tips

  • Study the entire scene before making a move

Look for switches, moving platforms, ladders, and guard patrol patterns before clicking anything.

  • Try using the pandas from smallest to largest

This helps you explore tight spaces first before bringing the stronger pandas into the puzzle.

  • Stack the pandas to reach higher areas

Climbing on top of each other is often required to activate elevated levers or access hidden paths.

  • Pay attention to timing

Some puzzles require you to wait for guards to turn around or for moving platforms to return.

  • Test nearby objects for interactions

Even background items can trigger mechanisms, open paths, or reveal puzzle solutions.

Advanced Strategies

  • Combine multiple actions at once

Move one panda toward a switch while another prepares to use a platform or rope to save time.

  • Distract guards to create openings

You can sometimes draw a guard’s attention to one panda while the others sneak past safely.

  • Use teamwork to cross difficult gaps

Throw and boost abilities help smaller pandas reach places they cannot access alone.

  • Adjust positioning carefully

Small movement changes can affect ropes, platforms, and interactive triggers.

  • Replay difficult scenes for smoother solutions

Repeating levels helps you discover quicker routes and more efficient puzzle sequences.
