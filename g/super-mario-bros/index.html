Super Mario Bros

Super Mario Bros is a classic platform game that helped shape the future of the genre. In this timeless adventure, Mario travels across the Mushroom Kingdom on a mission to rescue Princess Peach from the villainous Bowser. Featuring colorful 8-bit visuals, memorable music, and creative level design, the game continues to be enjoyed by fans of retro platforming games today.

Game Modes

  • 1-Player Game – Play solo as Mario (or Luigi after losing a life, depending on settings) and complete all eight worlds by defeating enemies and rescuing Princess Toadstool.
  • 2-Player Game – Two players take turns controlling Mario and Luigi, alternating after each life is lost or a level is completed until the game ends.

Gameplay

Take control of Mario as he travels through levels filled with hazards, enemies, and hidden rewards. Collect coins along the way to increase your score and uncover useful items that can help you progress.

  • Question Blocks

Question-mark blocks often contain coins, extra items, or special power-ups. Jump and hit them from below to reveal whatever is hidden inside.

  • Enemies

Face a variety of enemies, including Goombas, Koopas, and other creatures. Defeat them by jumping on their heads or by using power-ups such as fireballs. If you find a Super Star, you'll become temporarily invincible and can charge through enemies without taking damage.

Each world is divided into four stages, ending with a battle against Bowser. Careful jumps, smart use of power-ups, and quick reactions are essential as you work your way toward rescuing Princess Peach. Keep an eye on the timer at the top of the screen, as every level must be completed before time runs out.

How to Play

  • Move Through the Levels

Guide Mario across each stage while avoiding hazards, gaps, and enemy attacks.

  • Collect Coins and Items

Gather coins to boost your score and search for hidden rewards throughout the levels.

  • Hit Question Blocks

Jump into question-mark blocks to uncover coins, power-ups, and other useful items.

  • Defeat Enemies

Jump on enemies such as Goombas and Koopas to eliminate them, or use special power-ups to defeat them from a distance.

  • Use Power-Ups Wisely

Pick up mushrooms, fire flowers, and stars to gain special abilities and improve your chances of survival.

  • Reach the Goal

Make your way to the end of each stage and clear all four levels in a world to advance.

  • Beat Bowser

At the end of every world, face Bowser and overcome the challenge to continue your rescue mission.

  • Watch the Timer

Complete each stage before the countdown reaches zero, or you'll lose a life.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • Left / Right Arrow – Move
  • Down Arrow – Crouch (when Super Mario)
  • A Button (usually Z or Ctrl) – Jump / Swim
  • B Button (usually X or Alt) – Run / Throw fireballs (when Fire Mario)
  • Start (Enter) – Start game / Pause
  • Select – Select game mode (at title screen only)

Game Features

  • Classic side-scrolling platform gameplay with challenging levels
  • Explore multiple worlds filled with unique obstacles and enemies
  • Collect coins, hidden items, and useful power-ups throughout each stage
  • Use mushrooms, fire flowers, and stars to gain special abilities
  • Battle familiar enemies, including Goombas, Koopas, and Bowser
  • Four-stage worlds that gradually increase in difficulty
  • Hidden secrets and bonus areas waiting to be discovered
  • Simple controls that are easy to learn but rewarding to master
  • Memorable retro-style graphics and iconic soundtrack
  • Time-based levels that add extra challenge and excitement to every run

Developer

  • Super Mario Bros was developed by the team behind one of the most influential platform games ever created. Its innovative level design, smooth controls, and memorable gameplay helped establish many of the standards still used in platform games today.
