Tube Jumpers

Tube Jumpers is a fun local multiplayer game where up to four players compete to stay balanced on floating tubes. The challenge comes from the unpredictable obstacles that burst out of the water, including rocks, bouncing balls, and hungry sharks. Each hit can launch your character into the air, and you'll need quick reactions to land safely back on your tube.

The game features simple controls and fast-paced matches that are easy to learn but difficult to master. Whether you're playing against one friend or teaming up in group battles, every round is filled with chaotic and unpredictable moments.

Game Modes

Tube Jumpers includes multiple multiplayer modes that let players compete in different ways:

  • 1 vs 1 – Face off against a single opponent in a head-to-head battle.
  • 2 vs 1 – Two players team up against one player for an uneven challenge.
  • 2 vs 2 – Team-based matches where two players compete against another duo.

Each mode offers a different experience, encouraging players to adapt their strategy while trying to stay on the tube and outlast their opponents. The combination of changing hazards, physics-based movement, and competitive gameplay keeps every match unpredictable.

How to Play

Each player is assigned a single button to control their character. Press your button to move forward and try to keep your balance on the floating tube. Watch your position carefully and react quickly when obstacles knock you into the air. If you fall into the ocean, you're eliminated from the round. Stay afloat longer than your opponents to claim victory.
