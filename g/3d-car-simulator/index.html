3D Car Simulator

Developer: Faramel Games
Rating: 9.1/10
Released: October 2016
Game Engine: Unity WebGL
Platforms: Browser (desktop-only)
Orientation: Landscape
Car » Simulator »

3D Car Simulator is a casual driving game built around realistic car physics, large open maps, and relaxed free-roam driving. Choose your favorite vehicle, explore different environments, and create your own driving challenges with drifting, stunt jumps, speed runs, or simple cruising. The controls are easy to learn, making it a good choice for both casual players and driving fans. Whether you play on desktop or mobile, the game delivers smooth handling, responsive steering, and clean 3D visuals.

Gameplay

Select a car, load into a map, and start driving freely. The handling system balances grip and sliding, allowing players to learn smooth racing lines while also pulling off controlled drifts. Explore highways for high-speed driving, city streets for tight turns, and ramps designed for stunt jumps. There are no upgrades or complicated progression systems, so you can jump straight into the action. Players can also create personal challenges such as drift-only routes, no-brake laps, or timed runs across different landmarks.

Game Modes

  • Free Roam – Drive freely across three different maps without missions or time limits, allowing you to explore and enjoy the environment at your own pace.

How to Play

Press Play and choose your vehicle and map.
Start with slower driving to understand the steering, braking, and drifting mechanics.
Switch camera angles to find the driving view you prefer most.
Practice smooth cornering, drifting, and high-speed driving across different areas of the map.
Try different vehicles and routes to improve your best runs and driving control.

Keyboard Controls

  • W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys – Drive, accelerate, steer, and brake the vehicle.
  • 1 / 2 / 3 – Switch between the three available vehicles (rally car, police car, or muscle car).
  • Spacebar – Activate the handbrake.
  • C – Cycle through different camera angles.
  • Shift – Shift or adjust the vehicle.
  • R – Reset the entire game simulation.
  • G – Reset the position of the current car.
  • E – Turn the police vehicle lights and sirens on or off.

Tips & Tricks

Beginner Tips

  • Control the throttle carefully when leaving corners to avoid sudden oversteer and keep the car stable.
  • Use short handbrake taps to begin drifts, then steer into the slide while adjusting the throttle smoothly.
  • Test different camera angles to find the view that helps you judge corners and driving lines better.
  • Memorize map landmarks so you can create consistent routes for speed runs and time trials.
  • Brake before entering corners while the car is still straight to hit cleaner racing lines and smoother apexes.

Advanced Strategies

  • Chain drifts together by releasing the handbrake early and using throttle control to maintain angle through turns.
  • Try the Scandinavian flick technique by steering briefly in the opposite direction before turning into the corner for faster rotation.
  • Use trail braking by lightly holding the brakes into the apex to improve front-end grip and corner control.
  • Approach ramps diagonally to reduce airtime and recover faster after landing.
  • Test different vehicles on the same route to compare handling, acceleration, and top-speed performance.

Yes, the game features multiple vehicles and open maps designed for drifting, high-speed driving, stunts, and free roaming.

Can I save progress in the 3D Car Simulator?

The game does not use accounts or cloud saves. Players can track their own best times, routes, and driving challenges manually.
