Royale Dudes

Developer: Šarūnas Visockas
Rating: N/A
Released: January 2022
Game Engine: HTML5
Platforms: Browser (desktop, mobile, tablet)
Orientation: Landscape
Action » Multiplayer »

Royale Dudes is an online battle royale game where you face off against numerous players in a large combat arena. Your goal is to outlast everyone else and become the final survivor. Explore the map to find weapons, equipment, and useful supplies, then battle your way through increasingly dangerous encounters as the playable area continues to shrink.

What Are Royale Dudes?

Royale Dudes is a multiplayer battle royale shooter where players compete on a shrinking battlefield, gather powerful gear, and eliminate opponents to stay alive. Victory depends on quick reactions, smart positioning, and making the right decisions under pressure.

With colorful visuals, fast-paced action, and straightforward controls, the game offers competitive matches that are easy to learn and rewarding to master.

How to Play

  • Move around the map using the W, A, S, and D keys.
  • Aim and shoot with your mouse.
  • Collect weapons, ammunition, and health kits to improve your survival chances.
  • Stay inside the safe zone as the battlefield gradually shrinks.
  • Eliminate enemy players while avoiding incoming attacks.
  • Survive longer than everyone else to win the match.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • W / A / S / D – Move character
  • Space – Drift (while driving)
  • R – Reload weapon
  • E – Interact (enter vehicles, pick up items, access Buy Stations)
  • M / Tab – Enlarge/Open map
  • Q – Switch weapons
  • 1 / 2 / 3 – Weapon selection
  • X – Use special weapon
  • G – Throw grenade

Mouse Controls

  • Mouse Movement – Aim weapon
  • Left Mouse Button (1) – Shoot / Attack

Tips and Tricks

  • Use the top-down view to spot enemies and monitor nearby threats.
  • Take cover behind walls, buildings, and other solid objects during firefights.
  • Avoid staying in open areas for too long, as they make you an easy target.
  • Plan an escape route before looting or entering risky situations.
  • Stay alert while exploring and keep an eye on your surroundings.
  • If combat becomes overwhelming, retreat to cover and reposition before fighting again.
