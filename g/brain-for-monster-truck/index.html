Brain for Monster Truck

Brain for Monster Truck is a physics-based puzzle game where your goal is to drive a truck to the end of each level without dropping the cargo while collecting every star along the way. To reach the finish flag, you must draw lines that create safe paths and connect platforms across the stage.

Use smart planning and careful drawing to solve increasingly difficult levels. Create the right paths, grab all the stars, and clear each challenge one after another. Test your problem-solving skills and see if you can complete every stage.

Controls

Arrow Keys – Drive
Mouse – Draw paths

How to Play

Use the Arrow Keys or A / D to drive the truck. Click and drag with your mouse to draw paths across the level. Your objective is to reach the finish flag without letting the package fall off the truck. Try to maintain a steady speed so the cargo stays balanced while driving over your drawn lines.

If you make a mistake, use the eraser tool to remove unwanted lines and redraw your path. This becomes especially useful in later stages as the puzzles become more complex. Careful planning and smart adjustments are important for clearing the tougher levels.

Game Genre

Brain for Monster Truck may look like a driving game at first, but it mainly focuses on drawing and puzzle-solving mechanics. While you control a large truck, most of the gameplay revolves around creating paths that safely guide the vehicle through each level. Success depends more on planning and creative thinking than fast driving skills, giving the game a unique mix of puzzle and vehicle gameplay.

Difficulty Level

Brain for Monster Truck can become quite challenging as you progress through the levels. Players need patience, timing, and strong problem-solving skills to complete every stage successfully. Taking your time to plan each path carefully can make difficult levels much easier. If a drawn route does not work, simply use the eraser tool and try a different solution.
