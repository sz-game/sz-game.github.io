Skillfite.io

Skillfite.io is a multiplayer survival adventure game set in a massive open world that supports up to 100 players at once. Gather resources, craft stronger equipment, and improve your skills as you fight to survive and grow stronger. The world is filled with valuable materials, hidden discoveries, and dangerous encounters that reward exploration and preparation.

Choose your own path by joining forces with other players or venturing out alone. As you progress, you'll unlock better gear, strengthen your abilities, and craft powerful weapons to help you overcome tougher challenges. Whether you're collecting resources, battling rivals, or exploring new areas, every action helps shape your journey through this ever-changing world.

Gameplay

Skillfite.io drops you into a shared online world where up to 100 players compete, explore, and fight for survival. You begin with basic tools and must gather essential resources such as wood and stone. These materials can then be taken back to your base to craft equipment, weapons, and other useful items.

As you progress, you can improve your skills, create stronger gear, and decide whether to work alongside other players or battle them for valuable loot. The game features:

  • 6 trainable skills
  • 15 different resource types
  • More than 20 craftable weapons and armor pieces
  • PvP and non-PvP zones
  • Trading with other players

These features provide plenty of ways to develop your own playstyle and strategy.

How to Play

Start with Nothing

Begin your journey as a level 1 adventurer with no equipment or experience. Gather basic resources by chopping trees, mining rocks, and catching fish to improve your skills and stay supplied while fighting weaker creatures such as rabbits.

Gather Resources and Craft Equipment

Collect materials from the environment and use them to create weapons and armor. Wood can be used to craft your first set of gear, while mined resources like copper allow you to forge stronger equipment.

There are 15 different resource types available throughout the world, giving you plenty of crafting options as you progress.

Improve Your Skills

Every activity helps increase your skill levels. Reaching specific milestones unlocks access to better resources and equipment.

For example:

  • Reach Woodcutting Level 5 to harvest larger pine trees
  • Unlock access to stronger materials
  • Craft better gear as your skills improve
  • Earn useful loot from defeated animals

Explore Different Regions

The world features multiple areas to discover, each offering different levels of risk and reward.

You can explore:

  • Safe zones
  • Standard PvP regions
  • High-risk PvP areas

Use teleport points to travel around the map. Trading with other players is available in all regions, while high-risk areas allow players of any level to battle each other.

Controls

Keyboard Controls

  • W / A / S / D – Move character
  • 1 – 4 – Select hotbar item
  • E – Open crafting menu
  • Shift – Dash
  • Space – Interact/Attack

Mouse Controls

  • Left Mouse Button (Click) – Interact/Gather/Attack
  • Right Mouse Button – Dash
  • Scroll Wheel – Zoom in/out

Developer

Tips & Strategies

  • Adapt Your Tactics – Different opponents require different approaches. Adjust your strategy based on the enemy's gear, skills, and fighting style.
  • Take Advantage of the Terrain – Use the environment to create distance, avoid danger, or gain a better position during battles.
  • Balance Attack and Defense – Focusing only on offense can leave you vulnerable. Make sure your equipment and strategy provide both damage and protection.
  • Practice Regularly – The more you play, the better you'll become at recognizing enemy behavior, reacting quickly, and making smarter decisions in combat.
