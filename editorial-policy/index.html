Editorial Policy

At UnblockedGames.free, our goal is to provide a reliable and enjoyable browser gaming experience by publishing accurate game information, maintaining organized game pages, and continuously improving our content. This Editorial Policy explains how we review, update, and manage the information available on our website.

Our Editorial Principles

Everything we publish is guided by a few simple principles:

  • Accuracy before publication
  • Clear and easy-to-understand information
  • Regular content improvements
  • Helpful user experience
  • Respect for intellectual property
  • Transparency when updates are made

Our objective is to create game pages that help players quickly understand a game before they start playing.

How We Review Games

Before a game is featured on UnblockedGames.free, we review it to better understand its gameplay and basic information.

Our review may include:

  • Game genre and category
  • Core gameplay mechanics
  • Controls and supported input methods
  • Browser compatibility
  • Device compatibility
  • General user experience

This information helps us write clear descriptions and organize games into appropriate categories.

Content Standards

We aim to publish content that is:

  • Original and informative
  • Written in clear English
  • Easy to read on desktop and mobile devices
  • Regularly reviewed for accuracy
  • Useful for players looking for browser games

Where appropriate, pages may also include gameplay tips, controls, FAQs, and technical information to improve the overall user experience.

Content Updates

Browser games change over time, and we review our content periodically to keep it relevant.

Updates may include:

  • Improving game descriptions
  • Correcting outdated information
  • Updating screenshots or media
  • Refreshing FAQs
  • Adding newly available games
  • Improving page organization

Our goal is to keep information as accurate and useful as possible.

Corrections Policy

If we discover inaccurate or outdated information, we work to correct it as quickly as practical.

If you notice incorrect details on any page, you're welcome to let us know through our Contact Us page. Community feedback helps us improve the quality of our content.

Editorial Independence

Our editorial decisions are based on the quality and usefulness of content for players.

We aim to organize games and information in a way that helps visitors easily discover new titles and understand how browser games work.

Artificial Intelligence & Content Creation

We may use modern writing and research tools to help improve content quality, organization, and readability. However, every published page is reviewed and edited before it appears on UnblockedGames.free to help ensure it is clear, accurate, and useful for readers.

User Feedback

We value feedback from our community.

Players can contact us to:

  • Report incorrect information
  • Report broken game pages
  • Suggest new browser games
  • Share general feedback
  • Report technical issues

Constructive feedback helps us continue improving the platform over time.

Our Commitment

Our mission is to build a browser gaming platform that is easy to use, regularly updated, and focused on providing a positive experience for players.

As our library grows, we will continue improving game pages, expanding categories, updating information, and making it easier for visitors to discover quality browser games.

Thank you for supporting UnblockedGames.free
